Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This policy covers the website blackmarketapps.studio and the private social publishing tool that Black Market Apps (HA HOLDINGS, LLC) uses to post to its own social media accounts. Each of our mobile apps has its own privacy policy, linked on our home page.

The publishing tool

We run a self-hosted copy of the open-source scheduler Postiz to publish videos and posts to our own YouTube channels, Instagram accounts and Facebook Pages. It is used only by Black Market Apps staff, for accounts we own. It is not offered to the public.

When we connect an account, the tool receives an access token from the platform and reads the basic profile of that account (channel or page name, ID and picture). It uses the token to upload the videos, images and captions we write, and to read view and engagement counts for our own posts.

YouTube API Services

The tool uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to and read statistics for our YouTube channels. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page. Once access is revoked, or an account is disconnected in the tool, its tokens are deleted within 7 days.

What we store and share

  • Access and refresh tokens, account names and IDs, and the posts we schedule, stored in a database on our own computer.
  • Media files we upload, served from postiz.blackmarketapps.studio so the platforms can fetch them.

We do not sell this data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone other than the platform the post is published to. We do not collect data about people who view our posts beyond the aggregate counts each platform provides.

This website

blackmarketapps.studio is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics. GitHub may log visitor IP addresses for security, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.
